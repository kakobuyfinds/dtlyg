How to order through LoveGoBuy

  1. Open a CAFANS product page and use its Buy Link to check whether LoveGoBuy is offered. Keep the original marketplace link so you can verify the seller and product variant.
  2. Match the size chart to a garment or pair of shoes you already own. Record the selected color, measurements and seller price before checkout.
  3. When warehouse QC photos are available, compare the item label, color and visible construction with the option you ordered. Check the return window before approving shipment.
  4. Ask for a shipping quote using your destination and packed parcel dimensions. Compare the quoted billable weight, delivery estimate and insurance before selecting a route.

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is this LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

It is an independent product shortlist built from public CAFANS listings, with filters, links to the original product pages and a worksheet for order costs.

Where do the two main buttons take me?

Both open the CAFANS homepage. Individual catalog cards open their corresponding CAFANS product pages, where you can review buying options.

Do the catalog prices include international shipping?

The displayed figures are reference item prices in USD. Check domestic delivery, international shipping, agent fees and any applicable destination charges separately.

Can I order every item through LoveGoBuy?

Check the buying options on the CAFANS product page and confirm that LoveGoBuy accepts your exact listing, variant and destination. Seller availability can change.
